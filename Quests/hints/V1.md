# Hints · V1 · The Policy Engine

Spoilers! Use `python3 scripts/expedition.py hint <puzzle>` instead. It reveals one hint at a time and logs it for the Game Master.

## default_deny
1. With no rules, "is there a permit rule that covers you?" is no. Lean can just compute that.
2. `rfl` (both sides compute to `false`). `simp [authorize]` works too.

## forbid_wins
1. Unfold `authorize`. The result is false if the forbid part is true, and you have a witness: the rule `p`.
2. `simp [authorize]` turns the goal into "if some permit covers r, then some forbid covers r". Introduce what it gives you with `intro _ _ _ _` and look at the goal.
3. `exact ⟨p, hp, hf, hc⟩`: the rule, the fact it's in the policy, that it's a forbid, and that it covers r.

## permit_needed
1. "Allowed" means "some permit covers it AND no forbid covers it". You only need the first half.
2. `simp [authorize] at h` splits `h` into its two parts. Look at `h` in the Infoview.
3. `exact h.1`

## order_irrelevant
1. `List.any` over `p₁ ++ p₂` is "any over p₁ OR any over p₂", and OR doesn't care about order.
2. `simp [authorize, List.any_append, Bool.or_comm]`

## logs_never_deleted
1. Way 1: the last rule of `companyPolicy` is a forbid that covers every delete of the logs, so `forbid_wins` applies. Way 2: there are only three roles. Check them all.
2. Way 2: `cases role <;> rfl`. (`<;>` means "do the next tactic in every case".)
3. Way 1: `exact forbid_wins _ _ ⟨.forbid, fun r => r.act == .delete && r.res == .logs⟩ (by simp [companyPolicy]) rfl rfl`. Proving an engine property once paid off here.

## interns_read_only
1. There are 3 actions × 3 resources = 9 intern requests. Check all of them.
2. `intro a s`, then `cases a <;> cases s <;> decide`.

## change_leaks
1. The AI's new rule checks the role and the resource. What does it forget to check? Try some `#eval authorize aiPolicy ⟨.intern, ..., .code⟩`.
2. Interns can now DELETE code (and read it). Pick one.
3. `exact ⟨⟨.intern, .delete, .code⟩, by decide⟩`

## fixedPolicy
1. Keep `companyPolicy` and add ONE permit rule that covers exactly "intern, write, code". Your rule needs to test all three fields.
2. `companyPolicy ++ [⟨.permit, fun r => r.role == .intern && r.act == .write && r.res == .code⟩]`

## fix_is_exact
1. 27 requests: 3 roles × 3 actions × 3 resources. Take the request apart into its three fields, then check every combination.
2. `intro ⟨ro, a, s⟩`, then `cases ro <;> cases a <;> cases s <;> rfl`. If a case fails, your `fixedPolicy` changes a decision it shouldn't. Lean's error tells you which request.
