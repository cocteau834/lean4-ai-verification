# Hints · T0 · Proof Gym

Spoilers! Use `python3 scripts/expedition.py hint <puzzle>` instead. It reveals one hint at a time and logs it for the Game Master.

## even_sq
1. Replace n by k + k, then it's just algebra.
2. `rw [hk]` then `ring`.

## no_largest
1. h1 says N + 1 ≤ N. Which tactic knows that's impossible for natural numbers?
2. `omega`

## consecutive
1. Now the even number is n + 1, and it's the RIGHT-hand factor of n * (n + 1).
2. `exact h'.mul_left n`

## sum_evens
1. Both sides are polynomials in k. Is there a tactic that expands and compares them?
2. `ring`

## even_above
1. `Even m` means ∃ r, m = r + r. For m = 2n + 2, what is r?
2. `exact ⟨n + 1, by ring⟩`

## square_mono
1. You need a·a ≤ b·b from 0 ≤ a ≤ b, by multiplying two inequalities.
2. `nlinarith` (it multiplies hypotheses for you), or the exact lemma:
     `mul_le_mul hab hab ha (ha.trans hab)`.

## sq_zero_iff
1. Mathlib knows "a power is zero only if the base is zero". Search for it.
2. `exact?`, or directly: `exact pow_eq_zero_iff (by norm_num) |>.mp h`.

## dvd_chain
1. Unpack both hypotheses (they're ∃s), then give the witness k * m.
2. `obtain ⟨k, hk⟩ := h₁` and `obtain ⟨m, hm⟩ := h₂`.
3. `exact ⟨k * m, by rw [hm, hk]; ring⟩`
