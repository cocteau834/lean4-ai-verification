# Hints · A2 · Neural Net Safety Check

Spoilers! Use `python3 scripts/expedition.py hint <puzzle>` instead. It reveals one hint at a time and logs it for the Game Master.

## relu_nonneg
1. relu x = max x 0, and a max is at least each of its arguments.
2. `unfold relu` then `exact le_max_right x 0`.

## relu_of_nonneg
1. If x ≥ 0 then max x 0 = x. There's a lemma for exactly that.
2. `unfold relu` then `exact max_eq_left h`.

## relu_of_nonpos
1. The mirror image of the previous puzzle.
2. `unfold relu` then `exact max_eq_right h`.

## relu_mono
1. To show max x 0 ≤ max y 0, bound each argument of the left-hand max.
2. `unfold relu`, `apply max_le`, then `exact le_trans h (le_max_left y 0)` and `exact le_max_right y 0`.

## relu_sub_relu_neg
1. Two cases: x ≥ 0 or x ≤ 0.
2. `rcases le_total 0 x with hx | hx`, then rewrite with relu_of_nonneg / relu_of_nonpos (side conditions `by linarith`) and finish with `ring`.

## relu_lipschitz
1. Mathlib has a general fact about |max a c − max b c|.
2. `unfold relu` then `exact?` (it finds `abs_max_sub_max_le_abs`).

## robust_cat
1. Inside the box, neuron 1's input is always positive and neuron 2's always negative. Prove that, and the score becomes a linear expression.
2. `rw [abs_le] at h₁ h₂`, split each with `obtain ⟨h₁l, h₁u⟩ := h₁` (same for h₂), then `unfold score`.
3. `rw [relu_of_nonneg (by linarith), relu_of_nonpos (by linarith)]` then `linarith`.

## attack_exists
1. Search with #eval near the corners of the box.
2. (0, 0) works: `refine ⟨0, 0, by norm_num, by norm_num, ?_⟩`, then `unfold score`.
3. `rw [relu_of_nonpos (by norm_num), relu_of_nonpos (by norm_num)]` then `norm_num`.
