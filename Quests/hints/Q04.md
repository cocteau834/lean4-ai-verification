## boss
1. On paper: (k + 1)² = k² + 2k + 1 ≤ 2k² when k ≥ 4, and 2k² ≤ 2·2^k by the induction hypothesis.
2. Base: `norm_num`. Step: `rw [pow_succ 2 k]` then `nlinarith`.
3. Why `pow_succ 2 k` and not just `pow_succ`? Plain `rw [pow_succ]` rewrites the FIRST power it finds, which is (k + 1)^2 on the left. Naming the base and exponent points it at 2^(k+1). Try both and watch the goal.
