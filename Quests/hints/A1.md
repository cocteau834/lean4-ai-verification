## maxList_spec
1. Induction on the list. In the cons case, x is either the head or somewhere in the tail.
2. `induction l with | nil => simp | cons y ys ih => ...`, and in the cons case `intro x hx` then `rcases List.mem_cons.mp hx with rfl | hx`.
3. Head case: `simp [maxList]`. Tail case: `exact le_trans (ih x hx) (by simp [maxList])`.

## sumAcc_eq
1. The accumulator changes during the recursion, so the induction hypothesis must hold for EVERY acc.
2. `induction l generalizing acc with`, and for nil: `simp [sumAcc]`.
3. For cons: `rw [sumAcc, ih, List.sum_cons]` then `omega`.

## fastSum_correct
1. It's the helper with acc = 0.
2. `unfold fastSum`, then `rw [sumAcc_eq]`, then `simp`.

## average_replicate
1. The sum of n copies of c is n·c, and the length is n.
2. `unfold average` then `rw [List.sum_replicate, List.length_replicate, nsmul_eq_mul]`.
3. Then `have h : (n : ℚ) ≠ 0 := by exact_mod_cast hn.ne'` and `field_simp`.
