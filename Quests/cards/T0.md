## 🎓 T0 · Proof Gym: Autoformalization, by hand

**What you practiced.** Translating paper proofs into Lean, one sentence at a time.

**On the job.** Turning informal mathematics into formal statements and proofs is called *formalization*, and doing it with AI is *autoformalization*: one of the most active research areas in the field. Companies building AI provers employ formalizers to write Lean and, just as often, to check translations that AI systems produced.

**Level up.** Take one lemma you proved in a real course and formalize it. Ask the Game Master to check your statement.

**Résumé line.** “Formalized undergraduate proofs in Lean 4 (induction, cases, contradiction, existential arguments).”
