## 🎓 A2 · Neural Net Safety Check: Knowing what a guarantee covers

**What you practiced.** A robustness certificate for a tiny network, and an adversarial attack outside it.

**On the job.** Neural-network verification is a research field with its own competition (VNN-COMP). The key professional skill is precision about scope. Your certificate covers *one box around one input*, not stickers on stop signs in general. Debates about “guaranteed-safe AI” turn on exactly this gap between what's proved and what's needed.

**Level up.** Find the largest ε you can certify for `score` around (1, 1), and explain in your log why it stops there.

**Résumé line.** “Built a formal robustness certificate and an adversarial counterexample for a ReLU network (Lean 4).”
