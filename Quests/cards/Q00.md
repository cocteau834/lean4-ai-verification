## 🎓 Q00 · Orientation: The loop everyone lives in

**What you practiced.** Reading a goal, choosing a move, reading the error, and asking the library (`exact?`) when you're not sure.

**On the job.** Every Lean user works in this loop, from Mathlib maintainers to researchers training AI provers. Research engineers also read *thousands* of failed proof attempts from AI models. Knowing what a normal proof looks like is what makes the failures readable.

**Level up.** Play two worlds of the Natural Number Game, or the first chapter of *Mathematics in Lean*.

**Résumé line.** Not yet. Keep going. After Q02 you can honestly write: “Lean 4 theorem proving (tactic proofs, Mathlib).”
