## 🎓 Q04 · The Oracle Lies: Evaluating what AI claims

**What you practiced.** Refuting confident false claims with counterexamples, proving the true ones, and using the library.

**On the job.** Evaluation teams measure how often a model's claims survive checking, and whether its confidence tracks its accuracy (*calibration*). AI systems now also *refute* conjectures: DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus resolved dozens of OEIS conjectures with Lean-checked proofs. And “find the counterexample” is the fastest debugging move in all of research.

**Level up.** Give the nine claims to two different chatbots and score them. That's a tiny evaluation; put the numbers in your log (it's capstone practice).

**Résumé line.** “Formally verified and refuted AI-generated mathematical claims in Lean 4.”
