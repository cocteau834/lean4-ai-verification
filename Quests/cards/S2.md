## 🎓 S2 · Simpson's Paradox Lab: Aggregation can lie

**What you practiced.** Building and certifying an aggregation paradox, and proving the weighted-average identity behind it.

**On the job.** When AI provers are compared across benchmarks with different mixes of easy and hard problems, model A can win every difficulty bucket and still lose overall. Careful evaluation reports stratified results and explains the mix. This is the statistician's main contribution to AI evaluation, and it's rarer than it should be.

**Level up.** Look for a Simpson-style reversal in your mini-prover or capstone results (split by problem type or difficulty).

**Résumé line.** “Formalized Simpson's paradox and stratified-rate identities in Lean 4.”
