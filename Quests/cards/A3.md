## 🎓 A3 · Build a Mini-Prover: This is the job

**What you practiced.** An LLM-plus-Lean proving loop with error feedback, logging and cost tracking.

**On the job.** Research engineers on AI-for-math teams build this loop at scale: search over many attempts, learning from verifier feedback, retrieving relevant lemmas, and budgeting compute. Then they spend much of their time on evaluation and failure analysis, the part you just did by reading the CSV. Increasingly the problems are about code rather than math: proving that AI-written programs meet their specs.

**Level up.** Add one improvement (retrieval of similar solved problems, best-of-n, or escalating to a bigger model) and measure it on problems you didn't tune on.

**Résumé line.** “Built an LLM-guided theorem prover with Lean verification; measured success rates and cost across models.”
