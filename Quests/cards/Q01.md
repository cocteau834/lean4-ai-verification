## 🎓 Q01 · First Contact: The checker is the product

**What you practiced.** Using Lean both as a calculator and as a judge that refuses false claims, however confident the source.

**On the job.** This is the heart of “AI for math”. In reinforcement learning, a model proposes a proof and Lean compiles it. The reward is 1 if it checks and 0 if it doesn't. People call this a *verifiable reward*, and it's a big reason math and code are where AI reasoning has improved fastest: nobody has to trust the grader's opinion.
