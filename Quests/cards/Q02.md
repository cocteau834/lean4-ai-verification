## 🎓 Q02 · The Tactic Toolkit: Specifications are where the bugs hide

**What you practiced.** Core logic moves, plus *encoding* a word puzzle (knights and knaves) as a precise statement.

**On the job.** The encoding `x = true ↔ S` was a design choice. Get it subtly wrong and Lean will happily prove the wrong thing. Writing faithful specifications is the daily work of formal-verification engineers, and in AI safety it's the hard part: a proof is only as meaningful as the statement it proves.

**Level up.** Invent your own knights-and-knaves puzzle, encode it, and give it to your sibling. Did they read your encoding the way you meant it?

**Résumé line.** “Lean 4: proofs in propositional and predicate logic; formal encodings of constraint problems.”
