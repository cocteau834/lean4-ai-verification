## 🎓 V3 · Agent Guardrails: Safe for every possible agent

**What you practiced.** Modeling an AI agent's actions as a state machine, red-teaming a per-step guardrail with a sequence attack, separating safety from usefulness, and proving an inductive invariant: the guarded system stays safe for every sequence of actions, whoever chooses them.

**On the job.** AI agents now hold real permissions: email, code, payments. Safety teams build guardrails and monitors around them and red-team those guardrails, and research programs in "guaranteed-safe AI" (such as the UK's ARIA *Safeguarded AI*) aim to prove properties of the shield instead of trusting the model. Your `guard_always_safe` is that idea in miniature. Most guardrails today are tested rather than proved, so people who can do both are rare.

**Level up.** Add an `email (to : Contact)` action and a rule "never email anyone outside the allowlist". What goes in the state? Is it still an invariant of one state, or a property of the whole history?
