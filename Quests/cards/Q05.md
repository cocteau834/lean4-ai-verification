## 🎓 Q05 · The RH Audit: Auditing trust

**What you practiced.** The three checks behind the Riemann Hypothesis verification: it compiles, it uses no extra axioms, and the statement is really what it claims to be.

**On the job.** This is alignment work in miniature. An AI rewarded for “Lean accepts it” will exploit every gap it can find: smuggled axioms, `native_decide`, impossible hypotheses, a quietly weakened statement. Teams defend against this with axiom audits, independent checkers and statement-comparison tools, and benchmark maintainers regularly correct problems that were stated wrongly. Judging what a check *actually* guarantees is a career-long skill.
