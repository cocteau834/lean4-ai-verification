## 🎓 Q06 · Spec Review: Catching drift

**What you practiced.** Reviewing AI-written statements and code specs for fidelity: truncated subtraction, division by zero, too-weak specs that wrong code would also pass, and vacuous hypotheses. Then proving the evidence.

**On the job.** This is one of the most common real tasks in formalization and AI-data work. Companies training provers pay reviewers to read AI-generated Lean and reject statements that drift from the source. In AI-written software the same review happens on specs: a spec that says "sorted" but forgets "same elements" lets a function that returns nothing pass with a perfect proof. Your boss proof showed why it matters: Lean will happily prove a statement that says nothing.

**Level up.** Ask an AI to formalize five statements from one of your courses. Review them the way you did here, and log how many were faithful.

**Résumé line.** “Reviewed AI-generated Lean formalizations for fidelity; identified misformalizations and under-specified code specs (truncated subtraction, division by zero, missing permutation conditions, vacuous hypotheses).”
