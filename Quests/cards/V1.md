## 🎓 V1 · The Policy Engine: Verified access control

**What you practiced.** Modeling a system with types and records, proving properties of an engine for every possible policy, checking a concrete policy exhaustively, and reviewing an AI's policy change with a proof that it does exactly what was asked.

**On the job.** Access control is where formal methods have gone furthest in industry. AWS modeled its Cedar policy language in Lean and proved engine properties like "forbid beats permit", and it checks customers' access policies with automated reasoning (the Zelkova tool). Your policy diff in Boss 3 is a small version of the question those tools answer every day: does this change grant anything new?

**Level up.** Add a fourth role (contractor) and a rule with a time window ("weekdays only"). Which of your proofs survive unchanged? The engine proofs should, and noticing why is the point.

**Résumé line.** “Modeled a Cedar-style authorization engine in Lean 4; proved engine-wide properties (default deny, forbid precedence, order independence) and verified an exact policy diff for an AI-proposed change.”
