## 🎓 Q03 · Induction Dojo: Recursion plus induction is how software gets verified

**What you practiced.** Induction on numbers and on lists: proving properties of small programs (append, reverse, sum) and of a recursive function (`hanoi`).

**On the job.** Verified software is mostly recursive definitions plus induction proofs about them. This is how people prove properties of interpreters, compilers, protocols and authorization engines (AWS's Cedar is one, verified in Lean). Induction is also the pattern AI provers most often get *almost* right.

**Level up.** Prove a Fibonacci identity: `∑ i ∈ Finset.range (n + 1), Nat.fib i = Nat.fib (n + 2) - 1`. (Hint: state it without the subtraction first.)

**Résumé line.** “Verified properties of recursive list programs by structural induction (Lean 4).”
